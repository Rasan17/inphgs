# iNPHGS Calculator (idiopathic Normal Pressure Hydrocephalus Grading Scale)

A modern, interactive clinical grading tool for idiopathic Normal Pressure Hydrocephalus (iNPH) developed by **Dr G Narenthiran FRCS(SN)**.

---

## 📌 Clinical Grading Criteria

The iNPHGS evaluates the classic triad of iNPH across three separate domains with scores from 0 to 4 each (Total Score: 0 to 12):

### 1. Gait Disturbance (0 – 4)
- **0 - Normal**: No gait impairment; walks normally without instability.
- **1 - Complaint of instability**: Subjective unsteadiness or walking difficulty, but no objective gait disturbance on examination.
- **2 - Mild gait disturbance**: Objective gait impairment present (shuffling, widened base, turning instability), but capable of independent walking without walking aids.
- **3 - Moderate gait disturbance**: Requires walking aids (cane, walker) or personal support to walk.
- **4 - Severe gait disturbance**: Unable to walk even with aids; wheelchair-bound or bedridden.

### 2. Cognitive Impairment / Dementia (0 – 4)
- **0 - Normal**: No cognitive complaints or objective impairment.
- **1 - Subjective complaints / Apathy**: Memory decline or reduced spontaneity, but no objective daily impairment.
- **2 - Mild cognitive impairment**: Definite cognitive impairment, but fully independent in basic ADLs.
- **3 - Moderate cognitive impairment**: Requires partial assistance/supervision in daily living.
- **4 - Severe cognitive impairment**: Severe dementia requiring continuous total care.

### 3. Urinary Disturbance (0 – 4)
- **0 - Normal**: No urinary symptoms.
- **1 - Frequency / Urgency**: Pollakisuria, nocturia, or urgency without incontinence.
- **2 - Occasional incontinence**: Incontinence episodes < once daily (1 to several times weekly).
- **3 - Frequent incontinence**: Daily incontinence (≥ once per day, requiring pads/diapers).
- **4 - Complete incontinence**: Total loss of urinary control or catheter-dependent.

## 🚀 Features
- **Real-Time Score Calculation**: Instantly computes total score (0–12) with visual severity indicators.
- **Visual Triad Breakdown**: Interactive progress indicators for each of the 3 triad components.
- **Clinical Report Generator**: Generates formatted clinical notes for electronic health records (EHR).
- **One-Click Export**: Copy to Clipboard, Email report, and Print/Save PDF.
- **Dark / Light Theme**: Full theme support with glassmorphism and modern medical UI design.
